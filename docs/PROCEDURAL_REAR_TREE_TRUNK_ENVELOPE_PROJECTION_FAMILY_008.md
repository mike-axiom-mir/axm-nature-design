## Smallest reusable primitive

`axm.nature-branch-transition-trunk-envelope-projection-family/v0.1` owns only topology-free point projection:

1. resolve one exact authored trunk segment;
2. preserve a source-space sample's axial coordinate on that segment;
3. linearly interpolate the authored trunk radius at that coordinate;
4. project the sample radially to that analytic envelope;
5. report envelope and axial residuals plus projection span.

The retained family exercises three topology-free samples at each of the five Organic transition frames. The `north-low` compatibility gate separately replays Geometry PR #18's real eight branch-ring samples and requires the Procedural projector to match Geometry's existing projection result without inheriting Geometry's ring or bridge topology.
