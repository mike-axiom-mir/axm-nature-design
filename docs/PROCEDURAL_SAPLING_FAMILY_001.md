## v0.1 delegated degrees of freedom

The family may vary only:

- branch length scale around each branch's existing attachment point;
- branch yaw around that same attachment;
- leaf-blade length and width scale;
- leaf-blade yaw and pitch.

It may not vary:

- trunk points/radii;
- branch attachment points;
- flex zones;
- design-check ranges;
- donor provenance;
- environment or weather handoffs;
- source repo identity.

Every branch-associated leaf cluster follows the varied branch tip before its blades are varied.

## Determinism and provenance

A candidate RNG state is derived from the exact family digest, requested integer seed and bounded attempt index. Every accepted receipt records:

- exact base-source digest;
- exact family digest;
- seed;
- accepted attempt;
- exact candidate source digest;
- exact candidate mesh digest.

The base source must match the family contract byte-semantically through its canonical digest. A changed source fails closed rather than inheriting old variation evidence.

## Acceptance / rejection

Each candidate is rebuilt through the existing Nature mesh/evidence path. It must:

- keep the existing Organic Form structural/design checks passing;
- fit inside the retained west-A environment envelope `[2.2, 2.2, 5.2] m`;
- preserve all branch attachment points exactly;
- preserve source-owned immutable fields exactly;
- move at least four branch tips by at least `0.02 m`;
- change at least eighteen leaf blades by at least `2 degrees` on yaw or pitch.

Search is bounded to 32 attempts. If no candidate passes, generation returns `HOLD_NO_VALID_VARIANT`. The generator never widens the authored family to force a success.

Retained evidence seeds are `11`, `47`, and `101`; all must produce distinct source and mesh digests.

## Why this is separate from map procedural placement

`axm-map-design` already owns seeded variation of proxy transforms. This family does not change placement, path clearance, object/building authority, or map randomness. It changes only source-owned sapling branch/crown form within a fixed source contract. Any environment consumer must still re-test a chosen generated body in its receiving composition rather than inherit this family's PASS.
